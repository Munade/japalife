Japa Life privacy policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Japa Life is a single-player life simulation game.

What we collect

The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Live chat

Live chat is optional and for players aged 18 and over. If you join it, your in-game name, your character's look and the messages you send are sent through the public ntfy.sh messaging service to other players in the same place. Messages are public and kept by ntfy.sh for about 12 hours. Never share personal details in live chat.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, including the character name you type in, is saved only on your own device so you can continue playing. It never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact details on the Japa Life Google Play listing.
